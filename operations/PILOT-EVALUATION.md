# Online Session Rehearsal and Feedback Sheet

**Optional volunteer micro-pilot.** It is not a precondition for the current first-four-session registration. Use this sheet for a small rehearsal or after an actual class. Small samples inform adjustments; they do not prove course effectiveness.

## Educational check

1. Could participants name **one audible detail** (e.g. pause, repetition, articulation, phrasing)?
2. Could they distinguish an observation from a personal feeling, narrative or association?
3. Did they engage in understanding, discussion or experiment by microphone, chat or listening-only choice?
4. Could someone suggest a change in sound and compare the result?

## Technical check

1. Were music, speech, and Persian subtitles intelligible in Google Meet?
2. Did screen/tab audio work on the actual participant devices?
3. Did sequential speaking turns and fallback instructions work?
4. Did the facilitator retain enough time for participants' own responses?

## Simple participant exit questions (Persian)

1. «امروز به کدام ویژگی مشخصِ موسیقی بیشتر توجه کردی؟»
2. «در گفت‌وگو یا تجربهٔ موسیقایی، چه چیزی برایت روشن شد و چه چیزی مبهم ماند؟»
3. «آیا صدا، زیرنویس و امکان مشارکت آنلاین برایت مناسب بود؟ چه چیزی را بهتر کنیم؟»
