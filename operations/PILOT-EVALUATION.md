# Pilot Evaluation — Pedagogical and Technical Feasibility

**Status:** Instrument draft. Complete immediately after micro-pilot; do not treat it as proof of a full course's long-term effectiveness.

## Three independent success dimensions

1. **Audible learning:** participants identify an observable change (tempo, articulation, register, phrase ending, timbre) and separate it from inferred feeling/story.
2. **Agency:** participants can imitate or suggest one change, or design a response, without the facilitator prescribing all of the answers.
3. **Delivery:** everyone has a workable participation option; audio/video supports the intended comparison and the cohost can recover from a failure.

## Pilot go / revise / stop rule (proposed, not empirically validated)

- **GO to a longer test:** Most volunteers can supply at least one audible observation; each has a participation route; core audio contrast is discernible; no unresolved critical join or privacy failure.
- **REVISE and rerun:** Some volunteers hear the examples but instructions, interaction pacing or the example itself is confusing; a fix is identifiable.
- **STOP/CHANGE PLATFORM OR EXERCISE:** Participants cannot hear the relevant sound distinctions, cannot join reliably, or recording/rights/consent issues remain unresolved.

With 3–5 volunteers, interpret results qualitatively; do not claim statistical effectiveness. Seek at least one beginner and representative connectivity conditions.
