# Google Meet — Musical Audio and Accessibility Verification

**Chosen classroom platform: Google Meet.** This is not a Zoom/Skyroom selection exercise. If Meet cannot convey the required differences, evaluate a fallback *with Iran Musicology*; do not silently change the published platform.

## Setup and representative listeners

- Facilitator computer with wired or stable connection, headphones and microphone/instrument input.
- Iran Musicology host/cohost on a separate account and device.
- At least one listener on a representative network in Iran; ideally one participant on a mobile phone.
- Use **actual received audio** to judge clarity, not merely how the source sounds on the facilitator's computer.
- Do not put participant credentials, private meeting links, network identifiers or recordings into public GitHub.

## Test sequence

| Test | Method | Pass evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Join a test Meet using the actual instructions, including mobile | Participants can join with acceptable effort |
| Speech | Speak in Persian at class volume | Speech is intelligible without persistent echo |
| Recorded music | Present a Chrome tab containing an authorized 20-second audio/video clip and enable tab audio sharing when available | Important attacks, pauses and expressive contrasts survive transmission |
| Subtitled video | Play, pause and resume one verified excerpt with Persian subtitles | Subtitle readable on a mobile screen and aligned with sound |
| Live demonstration | Play original phrase twice, changing **one** performance parameter | Listener can identify the intended change |
| Interaction | One person finishes, a second responds after cue | Audible sequential exchange without collision |
| Weaker connection | Repeat on a less reliable/mobile connection | Core example is audible, or prepared fallback is effective |
| Failure recovery | Simulate lost screen share / facilitator disconnect | Cohost gives instructions and restores or substitutes activity |

Google Meet features and device UI can change; **test tab sharing with audio and any audio settings on the exact devices used**. Do not assume live instrument processing will always preserve musical sound.

## Fallback ladder

1. Retry the authorized clip via a tested alternative sharing method.
2. Demonstrate the musical contrast live from the facilitator's instrument/voice.
3. Ask participants to describe/choose a change in chat; facilitator performs the suggestion.
4. Cohost manages rejoin or communications if the facilitator drops.
5. If essential musical differences are inaudible, omit or reschedule the affected activity rather than pretending it worked.
