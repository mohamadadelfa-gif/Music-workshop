## Per-excerpt documentation

Create a record from [the excerpt template](EXCERPT-TEMPLATE.md) for each numbered clip, with an exact source URL, edition, timecodes verified against the particular video, transcription status, a short summary instead of long copyrighted quotations, Persian term decisions, and classroom task.

**Do not treat approximate timing in older planning notes as verified edit points.** Existing notes suggest possible clips from *What Does Music Mean?*: William Tell/story, timbre and single-note comparison, question/answer phrases, and piano/Dragnet contrast. Validate all against the chosen legal video copy before cutting or subtitling.

## Translation and media rules

- Cite speaker, program, original broadcast, and exact source/edition as appropriate.
- Mark transcription **draft / checked**, translation **draft / reviewed**, and subtitle sync **untested / tested**.
- Preserve distinctions between Bernstein's claims and the facilitator's own interpretation.
- Do not commit copyrighted video/audio or substantial transcript reproduction without suitable rights.
- Prefer links and short original pedagogical notes.
