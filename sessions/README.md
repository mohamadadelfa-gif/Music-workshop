# Session Plans | طرح جلسات

## Current candidate sequence (not a fixed syllabus)

The [revised proposal](../project/PROPOSAL-FA.md) covers the first four sessions of a continuing workshop:
1. Hearing and interpretation — distinguish audible features from feelings and imagined stories.
2. One small change — compare one expressive change and its perceived effect.
3. Listening and responding — let another person’s contribution shape a response.
4. Listening again and transfer — apply the questions to a new example and try one conscious change.

This is a proposed preparation outline, not a finalized syllabus. Harmony, form and motivic development can grow later; their full coverage is not promised in four meetings. Public framework: listen / understand / discuss / experiment.

## Standard activity sequence

**Listen → describe → test through imitation → respond to someone else → make one change → listen again → reflect.**

For each selected clip, use [SESSION-TEMPLATE.md](SESSION-TEMPLATE.md). The teaching plan should specify a beginner entry point, one optional challenge, and evidence of learning.

## First-session planning

Initial anchor candidate: *What Does Music Mean?*.
- Opening question: «از کجا می‌فهمیم موسیقی چه می‌گوید؟»
- Begin with a familiar recording; ask for associations without declaring one answer correct.
- Compare a heard feature with a proposed story or feeling.
- Pause and examine Bernstein's demonstration.
- Lead a short question-and-answer phrase exercise; replay and compare.
- Do not finalize running order or clip lengths until the exact video and participant exercise are rehearsed.

The older Nina Simone/*Hair* session plan remains background reference and should be adapted only when it improves this course.
