# First Online Session — Listening, Meaning and Musical Evidence

**Status:** Proposed lesson plan under [current Persian proposal](../../project/PROPOSAL-FA.md). Platform: **Google Meet**. Actual first meeting: **۲۵ آبان ۱۴۰۵** (announced); start time and duration **OPEN**.

## Core question

«چه چیزی در صدا می‌شنویم و چه احساسی یا داستانی از آن برداشت می‌کنیم؟»

Learners should distinguish one specific audible feature from their personal interpretation, and test one controlled change of a short original phrase. The broader educational framework is **شنیدن → درک → گفت‌وگو → تجربه**, not a requirement that everyone improvise or sing.

## 25-minute voluntary rehearsal / extract of the first lesson

| Minutes | Facilitator | Participant action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Meet sound and chat check | Indicate what audio is heard |
| 3–7 | Play an **authorized short, familiar** musical example | Name an emotion/image/association |
| 7–10 | Replay and ask what was *audible* | Identify a pause, phrase, repetition, contrast or other feature |
| 10–15 | Show one verified Bernstein selection with strategic pause, if ready | Compare Bernstein's question with their interpretation; ask what needs retesting |
| 15–21 | Perform **original** short melody twice with one changed feature | Propose a change, imitate privately if desired, or ask facilitator to perform |
| 21–25 | Return to original and distinguish observation/interpretation | Give one audible example and one personal reading |

### If Bernstein subtitles or rights are not ready
Use an original facilitator demonstration and the same guided listening activity; do **not** pretend an unverified segment is ready.

## Online participation

- Speak one at a time. Participant A completes a phrase before B replies.
- Other participants may imitate locally **with microphones muted**.
- Participation via chat, gesture, listening, or directing the facilitator is valid.
- Do not rely on simultaneous multi-participant singing/playing through ordinary Meet latency.

## Proposed full meeting development

Expand the short rehearsal into a full lesson **only after the final duration is confirmed**. Include welcome/audio check, first listening, one or two short Bernstein selections, facilitated experimentation, screen break, group discussion, repeat listening, and a short exit response. The prior 85-minute draft was an illustrative schedule, not an agreed class length.
