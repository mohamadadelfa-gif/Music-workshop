- Use the approved Iran Musicology slides as the visual precedent; do not guess their exact fonts, colors or margins without the originals.
- Respect Persian RTL composition, names, legibility and mobile text-safe areas.
- Make **online / Google Meet**, **first four sessions**, and the real registration route **@iranmusicology** clear.
- Announced start: **۲۵ آبان ۱۴۰۵**; precise four-session calendar, fees, capacity and meeting duration remain unconfirmed here.
- Teaching framework: **می‌شنویم، درک می‌کنیم، گفت‌وگو می‌کنیم، تجربه می‌کنیم**.
- Bernstein: instructional reference, not a formal event sponsor or partner.
- No public use of copyrighted Bernstein clips, music or portraits without suitable rights assessment.
