# Iran Musicology — Online Workshop Video Campaign

**Scope:** Exclusively the **Iran Musicology online music workshop** with محمد عادلفر as facilitator. **Do not use RIRA branding** in either video or infer this is a promotional campaign for another project.

**Status:** Scripts are drafts, not final approved public copy. Platform, registration link, dates, fees and exact Bernstein video usage remain open.

## Deliverables

| Deliverable | Target duration | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| 01 — Promotional Reel (تیزر) | 35–45 sec | Use an audible musical difference to spark curiosity and present the Iran Musicology online workshop |
| 02 — Presenter introduction (معرفی کارگاه) | 75–90 sec | Explain the teaching method, online participation, audience and intended learning |

**Shared creative hook:** Perform **one original short phrase twice**, holding notes constant while varying one controlled aspect of delivery (e.g., articulation). This allows a genuine listening question without using copyrighted Bernstein footage in an ad.

## Story arc — Promo (35–45 seconds)

1. Original two-version melody (0–6s).
2. Spoken question: same notes, different experience? (6–12s).
3. Present the interactive online workshop with Bernstein as reference (12–26s).
4. Emphasize no prior notation/instrument experience needed (26–34s).
5. Iran Musicology end card, facilitator name, confirmed CTA (34–45s).

## Story arc — Introduction (75–90 seconds)

1. Presenter introduces himself and online format.
2. Demonstrates a short musical contrast.
3. Explains Bernstein excerpt → pause → Persian discussion → imitation → responding → creating.
4. Explains online turns: listen together, respond by mic/chat, no forced simultaneous remote performance.
5. Defines beginner-friendly audience and expected learning.
6. Close with approved Iran Musicology CTA.

## Efficient production method

- Finalize both scripts and partner credits **before** recording.
- Record the short **original melody** with two clear differences and listen on phone speakers.
- Record presenter material for both videos in one session; short takes for easier correction.
- Assemble and review the promotional Reel first. Reuse shared footage and graphics in the introduction.
- Use 1080×1920 vertical master with readable Persian captions and platform-safe text positioning.
- Review readability in muted playback; review musical difference using headphones **and** phone speakers.
- Keep original source materials and project files private as appropriate; repo contains scripts, cue sheets and metadata, not restricted video/audio.
