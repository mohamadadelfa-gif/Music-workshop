## Shared opening event

Perform one **original** short melodic phrase twice, with the same notes and ideally the same tempo/approximate loudness; change **only one expressive dimension**, e.g. legato vs. staccato. Begin with the sound, then invite the listener to compare. An original recording avoids relying on unlicensed Bernstein music/video in public promotion.

## One-session production strategy

Finalize scripts and credit wording → rehearse original two-version melody → record both videos in one camera/light setup → make rough cut → verify transmitted musical difference on phone speakers/headphones → add approved graphic treatment and subtitles → final partner review → export.
